MINECRAFT / COMBAT / v1.0

Rocket RPGThe essentials.

A craftable rocket launcher with its own ammunition, straight-flying rockets, a huge explosion, and no firing cooldown.

Jump to crafting ↓

FIELD GUIDE

Getting started.

  1. Craft the launcher and RPG rockets using the recipes below.
  2. Keep rockets anywhere in your inventory. Each Survival shot consumes one rocket and one durability point.
  3. Hold the launcher and right-click to fire. There is no firing or reload cooldown.

FIELD GUIDE

Crafting recipes.

FIELD GUIDE

How it works.

  • Launcher durability: 384 shots. Creative requires no ammunition and consumes no durability.
  • Rockets fly straight with smoke and flame, detonate on blocks or entities, and expire after about four seconds.
  • Explosion strength is 20, compared with ordinary TNT’s 4. This is blast strength, not a fixed crater radius or guaranteed damage multiplier.
  • No fire is placed. Large terrain explosions can damage your builds. Try it in a test world first.

FIELD GUIDE

Install the right build.

  1. Choose exactly one jar that matches your Minecraft version and loader.
  2. Install the required loader. Fabric and Quilt Boat Motors builds require matching Fabric API; native Forge and NeoForge Boat Motors builds do not. Other mods here currently require Fabric API.
  3. Put the mod in your profile’s mods folder. For multiplayer, install it on the server and every client.
  4. Fully restart Minecraft to load the mod.

FIELD GUIDE

Versions & loaders.

The current build is for Minecraft Java 26.3 / Fabric, with Java 25, Fabric Loader 0.19.5 or newer, and compatible Fabric API. Forge, Quilt, NeoForge, and other Minecraft versions have not been validated for this mod.

FIELD GUIDE

Common questions.

Why will the launcher not fire?

Carry RPG rockets in your inventory and check that the launcher has durability remaining.

Where are the Creative items?

Look in the Combat creative tab.

Does RPG mean role-playing game?

Here RPG means rocket launcher. This mod adds a launcher and rocket ammunition.
