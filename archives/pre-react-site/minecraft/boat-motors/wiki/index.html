Boat MotorsThe essentials.

Give your boat a motor, fill the tank, and head out. Five horsepower tiers, engine sounds, and a live speed and fuel dashboard.

Jump to crafting ↓

FIELD GUIDE

Getting started.

  1. Craft a motor, then place a boat, chest boat, bamboo raft, or chest raft in water.
  2. Hold the motor and right-click the boat to attach it. New motors start empty.
  3. Hold a filled gas can and right-click the boat to add fuel.
  4. Board the boat. Use W to accelerate, S to brake or reverse, and A / D to steer.

FIELD GUIDE

Fuel & refuelling.

One full gas can holds one US gallon. Craft it from one empty can, coal, sugar, and blaze powder. Charcoal does not substitute for coal.

Motor sizes and tank capacities
MotorTankFinish
9.9 HP2.5 gallonsRed
20 HP4 gallonsBlue

100 HP12 gallonsBlack
  • Right-click with more cans to fill larger tanks. Fuel that does not fit stays in the can.
  • A completely used can becomes an empty can. Reuse it in the gasoline recipe.
  • Sneak-right-click with shears to remove the motor. Right-click with a different motor to swap it. Remaining fuel stays with the returned motor.
  • If the tank runs dry, you can still paddle normally.

FIELD GUIDE

Your dashboard.

MPH18
GAS
MPG4.5

Dashboard illustration. Values change while you drive.

  • MPH: your current speed.
  • GAS: a segmented gauge and gallons remaining.
  • MPG: estimated fuel economy at your current speed, not a guaranteed range.
  • HP: your attached motor’s horsepower.
  • Each motor has an original engine loop with pitch changing as you accelerate. Adjust it with Minecraft’s Friendly Creatures volume slider. The engine runs while occupied, fueled, and in water.

FIELD GUIDE

Install the right build.

  1. Choose exactly one jar that matches your Minecraft version and loader.
  2. Install the required loader. Fabric and Quilt Boat Motors builds require matching Fabric API; native Forge and NeoForge Boat Motors builds do not. Other mods here currently require Fabric API.
  3. Put the mod in your profile’s mods folder. For multiplayer, install it on the server and every client.
  4. Fully restart Minecraft to load the mod.

FIELD GUIDE

Common questions.

Why is my motorboat slow?

New motors have no fuel. Refuel, board the boat, and accelerate while in water.

Why is there still gas in my can?

The tank could not hold the whole gallon. The rest remains in your can.

Do I need a smaller motor to craft a larger one?

No. Craft each horsepower tier directly from its recipe.

Where are the items in Creative?

Look in Tools & Utilities.

How do I get the ingredients?

Smelt raw iron, copper, and gold. Mine redstone, diamonds, and coal. Harvest amethyst clusters in geodes. Craft sugar from sugar cane, blaze powder from blaze rods, and netherite ingots from four scraps and four gold ingots.
